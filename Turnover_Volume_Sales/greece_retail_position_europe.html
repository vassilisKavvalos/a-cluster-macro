6 · Evaluation

Cyclical position

Greece is currently in a Expansion retail regime with an RCI of +0.38. Its ranking of 6/27 shows where Greece sits relative to each country's own historical retail cycle.

Demand quality

The key discretionary signal is non-food ex fuel at +4.2% YoY, compared with food at +2.4% and fuel at -6.6%. This helps distinguish broad demand strength from defensive or category-specific resilience.

Structural five-year position

Since June 2021, Greek real retail volume is up +1.3%, while the nominal–real index gap widened by +26.0 points. The combination shows how much of the spending increase translated into real purchasing volume.
