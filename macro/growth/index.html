Growth & National Accounts

Beyond the GDP headline.

The Growth Hub decomposes quarterly national accounts into the sources and quality of economic expansion: consumption, investment, inventories, trade, productivity and labour-cost competitiveness. The emphasis is on composition, persistence and structural improvement rather than the headline growth rate alone.

Analytical framework

The reference country page is organised as a sequence from release diagnostics to structural interpretation.
01 · Current state

GDP momentum

Headline annual and quarterly growth, recent momentum and the current release context.

02 · Growth anatomy

Contribution analysis

Percentage-point decomposition of consumption, government, investment, inventories, exports and imports.

03 · Domestic demand

Consumption quality

Household demand, discretionary strength and the balance between consumption and capital formation.

04 · Capital formation

Investment quality

GFCF intensity and the productive composition of investment relative to GDP.

05 · External balance

Trade contribution

Whether net trade reinforces or offsets domestic growth and how imports interact with investment-led expansion.

06 · Structural quality

Productivity & competitiveness

Productivity, unit labour costs and EU-relative gaps used to test the durability of the growth model.
