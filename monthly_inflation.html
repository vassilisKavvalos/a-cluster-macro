The broad picture

A higher headline, driven mainly by energy

Euro area inflation increased to 3.3% in August from 2.9% in July. The rise was not shared evenly across components: energy climbed to 14.3%, while services eased to 3.0%, food was unchanged at 1.2%, and the narrow core measure edged down to 2.4%.

Reading the first estimate

August brought a sharper headline, not a uniform acceleration

The aggregate rise was concentrated in energy. Services and the narrow core measure both eased, and food inflation stayed low. At the same time, the country charts show that inflation conditions continued to differ substantially across the monetary union.
